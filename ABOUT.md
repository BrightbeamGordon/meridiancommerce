# About Meridian Commerce

## Overview

Meridian Commerce is a fictional B2B multi-vendor ecommerce platform designed for knowledge intelligence demonstrations. This website showcases the capabilities and features of this platform in a realistic business context.

## Company Profile

**Founded:** 2018
**Mission:** Make B2B commerce as seamless as retail
**Target Market:** Mid-market and enterprise B2B organizations including manufacturers, distributors, and wholesalers

## Global Presence

- **Operating Countries:** 87
- **Supported Currencies:** 23+
- **Supported Languages:** 42
- **Uptime SLA:** 99.9%
- **Customer Support:** 24/7

### Regional Offices

- 🇳🇱 **Amsterdam** - European headquarters
- 🇸🇬 **Singapore** - Asia-Pacific operations
- 🇨🇦 **Toronto** - North American operations
- 🇧🇷 **São Paulo** - Latin American operations

## Platform Capabilities

### Core Features

1. **Multi-Vendor Architecture** - Support multiple suppliers on a single marketplace
2. **Relationship-Aware Pricing** - Volume tiers, contract pricing, and buyer-specific rates
3. **Approval Workflows** - Multi-level purchase approvals and spending controls
4. **Multi-Currency Operations** - Real-time exchange rates and regional payment methods
5. **Tax Compliance** - Automated handling of EU VAT, US sales tax, and APAC GST
6. **Enterprise Integrations** - Pre-built connectors to ERP, WMS, accounting, and payment systems

### Platform Modules

5. Pricing & Promotions
6. Order Management
7. Payment Processing
